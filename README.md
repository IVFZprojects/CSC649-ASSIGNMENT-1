# CMS: Reducing Clinic Waiting Time with Machine Learning

> CSC649 Special Topics in Computer Science | Individual Assignment 1

**Summary:** This project studies why patients at small Malaysian clinics do not know how long they must wait. It proposes a Clinic Management System (CMS) that shows real-time queue status and uses machine learning to estimate waiting time, so patients can wait outside the crowded room.

---

## Problem Statement

Patients at small primary clinics in Malaysia cannot see how long they must wait. Clinics use paper tickets and call out names, so patients come early and sit in a crowded waiting area in case they miss their turn. This wastes their time, makes the clinic crowded, and causes stress. A study at a Malaysian primary clinic found the average waiting time was about 41 minutes (Ahmad et al., 2017).
